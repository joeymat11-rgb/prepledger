## Verdict
This is not a copy-editing problem. It is an information-architecture failure.
The shipped hub asks one page to be a notification center, diary, evidence monitor, room
directory, settings screen, and manifesto. The rooms then repeat the same mistake: answer,
explanation, inventory, provenance, and controls all occupy the same depth.
Replace the public-facing LEDGER with PROGRESS. Tier 1 — the answer: one calm Progress hub,
one viewport. Tier 2 — the focused room: one question per screen. Tier 3 — the proof.
Hub job: "See what your work has shown — and what still needs your say."

## The tier model + door contract
Every door: a plain name; one live insight or state; one 44px whole-row target. No
philosophy subtitles, no generic MORE. Every Tier-2 room uses the same shell. Tiers are
separate screens, never stacked accordions.

## The new Progress hub (as ruled: two cards + doors)
DECISIONS card (brass count, first pending summary, deep-link to expanded card, quiet zero
state) · LATEST RESULT card (newest engine-authored record entry, no invented ranking) ·
compact doors: WHAT'S NEXT · SLEEP · BODY · EVIDENCE (carrying the still-learning hint per
R6-3) · HISTORY · SETTINGS & DATA. Budgets: ≤1.05 viewports, ≤120 words, ≤7 headings, ≤11
content tappables, one primary action. Leaves the hub: the diary (→History), the 18-question
inventory (→Evidence/Still learning), room manifestos, the EXPERT label, Analyst (→Data
summary), Rules/Display/data ops (→Settings & data), the footer manifesto (→About).
