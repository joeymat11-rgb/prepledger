# The read

Eighth day, still nothing from you. Last weigh-in 8/21, last meal on paper 8/20, last session 8/21. The machine side keeps talking — your app counted the gap out loud again yesterday and now prices your loss rate anywhere from standing still to three-quarters of a pound a week. That interval includes zero, and that's the whole story tonight: the trend still says 162.9, the lowest it has ever been (my read agrees within a couple tenths), but it's a carried number. Nobody has measured you in over a week, and your own app is now openly saying it doesn't know whether you're still losing.

Worth saying plainly: nothing here reads like something went wrong. You got sick, you left a session early feeling rough, and the entries stopped. That's a person resting, not a person quitting. But the file can't tell the difference on its own — and this is the week where the difference starts to cost.

## What matters this week

**The free week is nearly spent.** The research on layoffs is friendly: under about two weeks, a trained lifter loses essentially nothing — strength and size come back like they never left. You're at day eight. Monday makes ten. Past the two-week line the losses stop being theoretical. Coming off a sick week the fix costs one line: log the sessions if any happened, or write "rest week" and be done. That single line turns an unreadable gap into a planned deload.

**The instruments have started to eat the gap.** Two things moved overnight, both machine-made, no input from you. Your app's maintenance estimate dropped about 200 calories in one step — not because your body changed, but because its measuring window slid over the empty days. And the "two slow weeks" rule it armed on Thursday is judging two weeks that straddle the wedding-and-sick water mask plus the read gap. The band is the app's call and I don't put numbers next to it. But both of those levers are currently being fed by instruments running on a dark file, and the fix for all of it is the same: a few clean mornings and honest meals re-price everything before any lever needs pulling.

**Two clocks, and one dies tomorrow.** The weekend bet — Friday 8/21 and Saturday 8/22 each over 2,600 — files void tomorrow if those two days stay blank. Rough numbers count; your own file says so. And the whoosh verdict — was the 8/19–8/21 drop fat or water — needs one morning read by Tuesday or it files void too. Every read that exists says fat. It needs one more to be official.

## Is lean safe

On everything logged: yes — but everything logged is now a week old. The receipts still stand. Protein has hit its window every week for twelve-plus straight, 174 average on the last logged week. Sleep debt was zero on logged nights. And your last two sessions were strong at the lightest you've ever been: hack met its target at 200, the hip thrust opened clean at 90, the press finally made its fourth set produce. Nothing in the file looks like muscle being spent. The honest caveat is the same as last week, one week heavier now: the pairing that actually spends lean is a big deficit with no training, and a dark file is exactly the file that can't rule it out. I have no evidence it's happening. I also can't show you it isn't. That's what entries are for.

## The one move

**Step on the scale in the morning.** Same move as the last brief, on purpose — it's still the single entry that does the most work. One morning read collapses the rate guess back to a number, settles the whoosh verdict, re-anchors the trend at or under the record, and hands the maintenance math a real data point to chew on. Everything else — the weekend fills, the rest-week line — queues up behind it.
