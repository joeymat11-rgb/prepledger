# Caselaw — rulings mined from behavior

Numbered rulings inferred from the athlete's own decisions in the feed/queue (never asked for). Format: facts (with numbers) · ruling · principle · scope. House law stands above all: deterministic engines compute, models interpret; everything measured-or-badged; every action is a consent-gated proposal; the coach holds structural authority.

**CL-1 · Press 245 is provisional on debt.** Facts: 245×8,8,7 landed 7/23, but on debt (fix-window opened 7/22); the prior clean 8,8,7 was 7/16. Ruling: does not count / no load until repeated clean (queue: "Do NOT load until owned"). Principle: reps earned on a debt day are provisional until repeated on a clean day. Scope: PR ownership + load progression. **Confirmed in full 7/30 (run 10):** 245×8,8,7 was repeated on a clean (non-debt) day and the app banked it — feed: "PRESS 245 OWNED — 8,8,7 repeated — that is the confirmation, so 250 enters the queue at coach flag." The intervening attempts behaved exactly as the ruling predicts: 8,7,6 (7/20), a debt-day 8,8,7 (7/23) that did not bank, 8,8,5 (7/27). Fourteen days from first landing to owning it, and the only thing that changed at the end was the day being clean. This also settles a competing story in my own briefs: the repeat happened after a 7.5 h night, but his history refuses sleep as the mechanism on this lift (8,8,7 after a 4.5 h night on 7/16; 8,7,6 after a 7.5 h night on 7/20) — sleep bought the clean week, CL-1 banked the reps.

**CL-2 · Cratered self-bump reverts.** Facts: leg extension self-bumped 150→155, cratered to 9,6 (7/21). Ruling: revert to 150; 155 reopens only after 150×9,9 lands. Principle: an unearned self-bump that craters reverts to the last owned load. Scope: load progression. **Satisfied and reopened 7/31 (run 11):** the reopening condition was 150×9,9 and he put up 150 × **10,9**; the app wrote "EXTENSION 150 RE-OWNED — 9,9 — the 155 gate reopens, earned" and `q_ext155` entered the queue as DEBUT. The full arc is the cleanest vindication of this ruling on record — self-bumped 155 cratered 9,6 (7/21) → reverted to 150 → 6,9 (7/24) → 8,7 (7/28) → 10,9 (7/31) → gate reopens. Ten days from crater to earned re-entry, and the thing that closed it was an opener taken harder rather than any change in load. **The ruling's live half is now the debut itself:** 155 has one prior data point and it is a crater, so this attempt is what tells us whether the crater was the load or the unearned-ness. Carded as `sug_2026-08-01_ext155` with an explicit success threshold (≥8,8) so a normal +3.3%-load debut is not mistaken for a second crater and self-reverted.

**CL-3 · Sleep gates structural additions. — RETIRED BY THE APP, 7/29. Do not cite as live.** Facts: hack 3rd-set debut passed its gate but was deferred 2× for sleep; now runs unless a true <4.5h night. Ruling (as it stood): sleep quality can defer an already-earned progression. Principle: recovery state gates structural additions, even earned ones. Scope: session sequencing + recovery. **Struck 7/29 by the app itself**, in a feed entry titled "THE CLEAN-SLEEP GATE IS GONE": *"It was retired in the engine earlier but survived in three places that decided what you lift: the prescription desk held every lift on a short night, the set plan pulled your last set off failure, and the queue still told you sleep decided before your muscles did. None of that had evidence behind it — a short night costs about 2.85% on strength, which is smaller than your own set-to-set spread. Sleep still matters enormously, just for a different thing: at a matched deficit it decides how much of what you lose comes off muscle instead of fat. It is now on your sleep card as that, and nothing anywhere gates a rep on it."* Noted 8/1 (run 11). This is the first ruling in this file to be **overturned by a change in the system rather than by the athlete's behaviour**, and it matters beyond calves: [[CL-10]] was mined partly against CL-3's logic (the hold card that CL-10 examines was "CL-3 applied off recovery 45/100"), and my own 7/30 restraint leaned on the CL-1-not-CL-3 distinction. Those remain sound — CL-10 is about *authority*, not about whether sleep should have gated anything — but any future card that wants to defer an earned progression on recovery grounds no longer has CL-3 to stand on and must argue from the data directly. Sleep's standing in the constitution (Article VI, Nedeltcheva 2010) is untouched: it is a body-composition lever, not a rep gate.

Ruling: pre-agreed social/event meals incur no compensatory deficit. Principle: sanctioned events are not tracked at the table and not repaid. Scope: nutrition + event days.

**CL-7 · An ascending rep pair is not a transposition.** Facts: extension 7/24 logged 150×6,9 (second set above the first) with NO contradicting note. Asked "real or transposed," the athlete ruled "Real" (feed ANALYST ANSWER, 7/25). Ruling: the ascending pair stands as logged — a cautious opener followed by a stronger second set is legitimate. Principle: a second set higher than the first is not, on its own, a data error; only flag a rep-shape surprise when a note or day-flag actually contradicts it. Scope: log integrity. Distinguishes [[CL-6]]: CL-6 required a note-vs-log contradiction; here there was none, so the log is trusted intact.

**CL-8 · An RIR-buffer override never launders debt into owned reps.** Facts: on 7/24 the athlete fired the "RIR DEBT BUFFER — OVERRIDDEN" call ~11 times (athlete call, this session only); the app held banking unchanged and every set still logged provisional. Ruling: the athlete owns effort/RIR on the floor, but overriding the buffer does not bank the reps — debt-day and provisional sets stay provisional. Principle: floor autonomy over effort is not banking authority; consent to push does not convert provisional reps to owned load. Scope: RIR + banking + load progression. Reinforces [[CL-1]] (debt reps are provisional until repeated clean).

**CL-9 · Estimate days are declared post-hoc, at the day level.** Facts: Wedding #2 (7/25) had `dayCtx["2026-07-25"].est` set true with note "declared estimate day," while `events[wed2].estimated` stayed false — the flag went on *after* the event, on the day, not the event, and not in advance. Two analyst pitches to pre-declare the estimate day (7/24, 7/25) were both dismissed. Ruling: the athlete owns the estimate flag and sets it retroactively after the meal is behind him; the event-level `estimated` field is not his lever. Principle: estimate days are a post-hoc, day-level declaration by the athlete — never pre-emptive, never event-level. Scope: estimate days + event accounting. Complements [[CL-4]] (sanctioned events carry no penance): CL-4 governs the no-comp math, CL-9 governs *when and where* the flag is set. **Third occurrence 8/1 (run 11), and the first without an event attached.** `dayCtx["2026-07-30"]` carries `est: true`, note "declared estimate day" — set after the fact, at the day level, on a Thursday with no wedding, refeed or entry in `events[]` behind it. The day logged 3,200 kcal / 200 g protein / 19,000 steps / sodium high. So the flag is not an event mechanism at all: it is how he marks any day whose numbers he does not trust, and he reaches for it retroactively. Two consequences for how I read him. Any `est: true` day should be treated as a *number with wide error bars*, not as a licence to discount it — the 3,200 still moved the week's mean 240 kcal and still produced a +4.0 lb scale response the next morning. And [[CL-4]]'s no-penance logic extends here by analogy but not by right: 7/30 was not a sanctioned event, so the reason not to repay it is arithmetic (one day against a measured maintenance) rather than the standing zero-comp rule.

**CL-10 · Consent to a hold does not bind the floor; a self-run structural debut stands, but provisional.** Facts: on 7/28 the athlete APPROVED `sug_2026-07-28_hold` ("hold the Calves 320 debut until a clean week" — itself CL-3 applied off recovery 45/100), then the same cycle ran Calves 320×10,8,7,7; the engine advanced `q_calves_inc` to ESTABLISH/done rather than reverting it, on a non-clean week (sleep debt, recovery-low proposal `recovery_2026-07-27` stood down). Status 7/30 (run 9): still open and still untested — 7/28 remains the only session at 320, so the "kept but provisional" clause has had no repeat to be judged on, and the QUESTION is unanswered.
