# COACH EXPERIENCE — the product brief (owner rulings 2026-09-12, DECISIONS:139–140)

The rules of the coach do not change (DECISIONS:89, C5 TOOL-CONTRACT.md): voice is the mouth, the engine is the brain;
every spoken number traces to an engine tool result in the same turn; the model never authors plan numbers; plan changes
only as engine proposals accepted on a yes; tier-3 topics refuse; no medical advice; opt-in that names audio/text leaving
the phone; hard spending cap before the first live call; no server-side recording. This brief says what the coach DOES
with those rules, ranked, so lanes build toward one product moment rather than a tool list.

## The moment (owner, verbatim)
"launching the app and easily talking to the coach about what im hitting that day, randomly ask it for settings on a
machine" and: "For a beginner this is the moment it stops feeling like an app" is the whole point.
Demo script, which is also the acceptance bar for wave one, first in text (C5 prototype), then by voice on the phone:
1. Open the app. Today shows; one tap on "Talk to Earned" (or a greeting if opted in).
2. "What am I hitting today?" → the day read back from the engine (kind, lifts, sets, targets) with the WHY for anything
   that changed ("rows went up: you hit the target two sessions running").
3. "What's my seat on the chest press?" → "Seat four, pin three" from stored facts; if never captured: "I don't have it
   yet; tell me while you're there and I'll keep it."
4. "One-ten for eight." → logged as set N through the gym card's write path; confirmation aloud; next set named.
5. "Why is today lighter than last week?" → the engine's stored reason, in one sentence.

## Wave one (the demo) — build first, ship before wave two
1. TALK ABOUT TODAY: plan read-back with the why (needs P6 reason-on-disk for the why of past changes).
2. MACHINE SETTINGS + FORM CUES: captured by voice the first time ("seat four, pin three") and stored as FACTS on the
   phone (tier 1, the check-in's fact/event mechanism; no engine change; H2's engine member can come later); recalled on
   ask; the form cue read before a set when the lift carries one.
3. HANDS-FREE LOGGING in the same conversation: weight and reps → the accepted gym-card write path; effort words map to
   the existing effort options; "undo" = the accepted durable removal edit.
## Wave two
4. MORNING CHECK-IN by voice: weight, sleep, how you feel → the existing weigh-in and check-in commands; then today's
   plan in one breath.
5. SESSION DEBRIEF after the last set: what moved, what to expect next time, two sentences, engine facts only.
## Wave three
6. PAIN AND SORENESS handled through the recovery check-in; plain statement that it is not a doctor.
7. PROGRESS in his own numbers, weekly, one sentence, engine facts.
8. "TEACH ME" mode from a vetted glossary (public vocabulary, same answer every time).
9. GYM-PROOF PRACTICALITIES: short replies, interruptible, a text mode for loud gyms, a visible listening indicator,
   everything but the voice works offline.
10. VOICE ONBOARDING (C6, DECISIONS:134/:139): the six setup questions by voice; optional; never a gate.

## Rule for the lanes
Nothing from a later wave starts before the earlier wave's acceptance bar is green on a real phone; the owner's and
Dad's reactions to wave one re-rank waves two and three. Owner-facing copy: no em or en dashes (DECISIONS:114).
