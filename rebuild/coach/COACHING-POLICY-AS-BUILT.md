# PART 1: FOR THE OWNER

## WHEN EARNED CHANGES A WORKOUT

Earned advances ordinary rep targets from recorded effort.
On multi-set lifts, last-set effort budgets extra reps: failure adds 1, 1-2 left adds 2, and 3-plus left adds 3 across eligible sets.
Heavier weights normally need 2 qualifying top-range sessions running, or one improvement beating the noise threshold.
Two recorded first sets at failure hold the load until one leaves at least 1 rep.
Three repeat-or-worse comparisons within the last 5 sessions can trigger review; a load hold or poor recovery permits a reset offer.
More sets need the engine's go-ahead, checked 7 days apart, and predicted growth of at least 2.05 percentage points.
Example: with no effort ratings, the rep budget defaults to 1, subject to target ceilings.

## WHEN IT HOLDS OR MOVES CALORIES

Calories follow food logs, weight change and the chosen loss rate, with a lower limit.
That limit is 25 calories per kilogram of estimated lean mass plus estimated training cost, rounded to the nearest 50 calories.
The calorie-adjustment calculator proposes nothing if the latest weigh-in is at least 3 days old, uncertainty covers the target rate, or the correction is below 90 calories daily.
Falling lifts and weight can raise the day's food instruction toward maintenance, immediately for severe decline.
Example: a noisy weigh-in cannot justify a correction while uncertainty still covers the target.

## WHEN IT ASKS YOU SOMETHING

The coach needs your yes to record a fact or accept an engine-issued proposal.
The check-in asks about sleep, energy, soreness, stress, pain, illness and time away.
Selecting mild/significant soreness, pain, illness or time away opens follow-ups.
One check-in saves per day on the device; tomorrow starts blank.
Example: last night's recorded sleep is offered for confirmation, not asked again.

## WHEN IT SAYS IT DOES NOT KNOW

Missing check-in answers stay unknown.
The coach takes its numbers from this turn's tool results.
An unmeasured weight-change rate stays blank; no qualified past workout means no comparison.
Before maintenance is measured, estimated calories start at 12 per pound of bodyweight, minus the planned cut, with the lower limit applied.
Example: without bodyweight either, there is no calorie target.

## WHAT IT RECORDS TODAY AND THEN IGNORES

These reports do not change the plan.
Confirmed pain and soreness save in the daily check-in and show back; nothing happens to the plan.
Time away saves days and reason for read-back; nothing happens to the plan.
The missing-equipment tool cannot record the report; nothing happens to the plan.
Example: reporting an unavailable machine does not produce a replacement exercise.

### DECISIONS ONLY YOU CAN MAKE

- Keep the 90-calorie adjustment cutoff, change it, or require stronger evidence?
- Keep one rep-step rule for beginners and experienced lifters, or distinguish them?
- Should pain only be recorded, pause the affected exercise, or ask for human review?
