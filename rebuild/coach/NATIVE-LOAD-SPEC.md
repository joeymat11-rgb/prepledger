### Queue coexistence (R2, answers a7b0e24 section 4 and N3)
a7b0e24 asks "whether an untaken two-rung offer remains beside an accepted rung and how import-merged pairs are represented." Answer: (1) native offers are evaluation outputs, never queue entries, so an untaken native alternative never sits in the queue; at most ONE unresolved native entry per lift exists (TARGET_QUEUED otherwise). (2) Imported or merge-minted legacy entries, pairs included, are preserved byte for byte in their order and are never superseded, tapped or declined by native code; the lift returns LEGACY_PENDING (below).
