# Three proposed coaching choices

Prepared after the completed science review and E380 correction contract; PM388. None is approved yet. These choices change future app behavior. Existing records, accepted plans and safety/recovery protections remain. They do not authorize deployment or importing private data.

1. **Keep advice tied to your chosen goal, and require review before changing goals or adding sets from a research formula? Recommended: Yes.** A weight or strength pattern would raise a review note, rather than silently change a cut to maintenance or a surplus. Planned sets still display; the research curve stops producing a personal gain forecast or an automatic set increase. A different goal or programme still needs the existing explicit approval route.
2. **Make one clean rep left the normal final-set target, leaving all-out sets to an explicit choice? Recommended: Yes.** A normal three-set exercise becomes two, one, one reps left; a one-set exercise starts at one. Existing protective exceptions still apply. The app records what you actually did, including an all-out set, and does not rewrite old workouts. This is an operating default, not a claim that one rep is everyone's optimum.
3. **Use one consistent energy estimate for warnings, and base routine diet-break suggestions on a recent pattern of low energy and eating above plan, with sleep checked? Recommended: Yes.** The warning uses the existing structured-exercise estimate consistently. A routine one-week maintenance-break suggestion needs the last seven completed days, at least four energy and four food entries, three days above a stable accepted target, and three qualifying sleep nights. Missing evidence means review, not a claim that everything is fine. You can still request a break or help without waiting to fill every log. The specific window and coverage counts are product choices, not clinical thresholds.

Each answer is independent. Full proposed behavior, retained exceptions and 23 unexecuted examples: [CASES.md](../e/science-correction-contract/CASES.md). A No or no answer holds that proposed behavior; it does not undo the settled accuracy and missing-data fixes. Implementation and independent tests are still required after approval.
