# FRICTION LOG - Earned on Joe's phone

Opened 2026-09-16. Source chat: the "friction log" cowork chat (this account). Owner: Joe.

## How this works
- Joe uses Earned on his phone (earned-slice.netlify.app, Add to Home Screen) and drops anything that annoys, confuses, blocks or surprises him into the friction-log chat: a sentence, a screenshot, a voice-typed rant. No format required.
- The friction-log chat records each item here as one numbered entry (F-001, F-002 ...), in Joe's words plus what he expected, and gives it a first triage. It does not build, dispatch or merge anything.
- The PM chat (day-to-day PM per DECISIONS:414) reads this file, turns entries into tickets, routes them, and writes the outcome back in the Status line. Joe is not the messenger between the two chats; the file is.
- Severity: BLOCKER (cannot log or read something he needs today), HIGH (works but costs him time or trust every session), MED (annoying, workaround exists), LOW (polish, taste), IDEA (not a defect; a wish).
- Owner taste calls stay Joe's: an entry marked TASTE is logged, not decided, until he rules in his own words.
- Entries are never deleted. A fixed entry keeps its line and gets Status: FIXED (package name, ledger line).
