# Earned refinement A — owner-approved design direction

Joe reviewed the three rendered screens and approved them with: "Yes that looks good". This approves the visual and interaction direction for integration into the retained rebuild. It does not approve sample prescriptions, qualify the prototype as product code, waive technical review, or add new release blockers. No retained app or original approved mock has been modified by APM.

## Plan before build

Preserve the recorded paper/ink aesthetic: paper #F4F0E8, ink #1C1B18, secondary ink #5A5348, hairline #D8D0C2, green #2E5A3C, ground #E7E1D4. Instrument Serif carries headings and prescriptions; Instrument Sans carries evidence, controls, and small numeric inputs.

Today moves the agreed daily plan ahead of the scale measurement. Layout: quiet date/brand, plan instruction, unboxed food targets and workout, supporting weight trend, primary start/resume action. Use one column, shared left alignment, and hairlines instead of container cards.

Gym moves actual load editing alongside reps, shows the current set's prescribed load/reps/effort separately from what was performed, provides optional last-time context, and retains the full-width logging action. A saved-set/rest view makes the next step clear without relying on a transient toast. Direct number entry and steppers serve both small and large corrections. Planned values never become completed facts until the user explicitly logs them.

The same-serif/cream combination is deliberately retained because it is the owner's approved direction. The refinement changes task hierarchy and interaction rather than replacing its visual identity. No streaks, ratings, gamification, badges, decorative gradients, or new dashboard panels.

## Scope and interpretation

All dates, numbers, history, prescriptions, workout duration, progress explanations, and rest values are fictional examples. They are not generated by the engine and are not advice for Joe or Dad. The prototype uses temporary in-memory interaction only. Reload resets it. Saved labels are simulated states within this clearly labeled design preview. Buttons never contact Earned, create records, or save health data.

Views: Today before/after a workout starts; active Press set; saved set with rest and Undo; explanatory disclosures. Screen review controls outside the phone can reset scenarios or enlarge text. A snapshot of the target is visually distinct from the performed entry fields. Unknown effort is an explicit choice and no effort rating is preselected.

Rationale example uses an agreed rep increase at unchanged load, not an automatic plan change. Detailed history compares only synthetic equivalent setup/load. The rest timer is an interaction proposal, not a new first-use release requirement. Longer-term changes, permission/consent, real storage failure, authentication, recovery, and engine rules continue to follow the accepted product process; this prototype does not qualify them.

## Owner decision

Approved: reduced scale prominence, prominent daily plan, direct weight/reps fields, and the saved/rest transition within the existing paper-and-ink aesthetic. Preserve the original approved design as historical provenance. The new direction applies to the three demonstrated states, not every unshown state or historical behavioral rule. Earned owns the implementation and applicable source/behavior review.
