## 0. Authority, purpose and scope

For the owner: this work makes “Saved” mean the entry and its sending queue finished saving together. After the app restarts without internet, the allowance remains **24 observed hours and 64 new operation slots since its last reconciled connection**, whichever runs out first. A weigh-in uses one slot; a multi-part action can use several. Reopening does not refill the allowance. At the limit, or after a detected clock rollback invalidates that allowance, the app keeps the entered values and asks you to reconnect. The recorded rollback default is refusal until reconnect; the owner may override it.
Version 1.1 implements Sol's recorded **ADOPT-WITH-CHANGES** verdict, [SOL-W6-REFINEMENT-2026-09-06.md](SOL-W6-REFINEMENT-2026-09-06.md) and DECISIONS at `cd99918`; cowork must verify this diff before marking W6-REFINEMENT DONE. The retained 64 slots are a **client-policy cap only, never a loss bound**. The owner already chose bounded offline saving over blanket refusal after restart (DECISIONS, 2026-09-05, accepted at `0a13921`). Do not ask that choice again. This is not a guarantee of 24 real hours or at most 64 lost entries: restoring an old, internally consistent copy can restore its old allowance too. That restore exposure is the accepted residual, not an open strict-versus-bounded decision.

### Bounded restart refinement v1.1 (retained numbers; four recorded corrections)

**Scope:** for an intact, enrolled, decryptable installation after unproved continuity, apply this allowance in addition to every issued lease/schema/sequence and standing constraint. Preserve the sheet's original lease term (A2:136–149, including the minimum 30-day issued capability); this shorter client restart allowance is a separate refinement, not an early server revocation. Qualified continuous-execution behavior retains its existing lease constraints. Count all new committed slots since the checkpoint, including those written before the restart, so restarting cannot create another pool.
**Checkpoint C:** after an authenticated, reconciled exchange, atomically store its identity/version, signed time/lease proofs, enrolled device and key/schema epochs, verified frontier/head, local pending lineage, next sequence, wall sample W0 and last persisted sample W_last=W0, nondecreasing observed-time high-water H=0, and used slots U=0. Define reconciliation against W5's published proof; it may preserve outstanding WAITING/unacknowledged operations with their original identities. A token refresh, successful GET /time alone, page load, install, clock edit or local snapshot restore never issues C.
**Observed elapsed:** on a bounded-path decision, sample UTC wall milliseconds W; reject nonfinite readings. A detected wall rollback `W < surviving W_last` explicitly INVALIDATES/EXHAUSTS the restart allowance: refuse state 20 by definition of that allowance, even when its numeric time/slot counters remain below their limits. This is not proof of lease expiry or an additional unstated refusal. The recorded default requires authenticated reconciled reconnect before a new allowance; the owner may override that default. Charge `H = max(previous H, W - W0, 0)` in milliseconds. After an unproved restart H is observed wall advance and can undercount elapsed/server-clock advance. A separately qualified same-execution elapsed upper bound or authenticated newer evidence may only tighten permission; it must not be claimed from an unqualified performance timer. Persist H and W_last=W with each acknowledged update atomically; preserve detected allowance invalidation through the same fence/recovery requirements as other learned refusal knowledge. Record/check proven expiry and recovery fencing as below, including failures to persist that knowledge.
**Lease-time mapping — CONDITIONAL:** checkpoint C also records the authenticated server-time interval [Tlo, Thi]. Only under W5's published freshness, server-error and server-clock-rate assumptions sufficient to justify this mapping may `max(surviving lease-time high-water, Thi + H)` be used as a bound against lease.not_after; those assumptions remain OPEN. At checkpoint creation require Tlo ≥ lease.not_before under that authenticated interval profile; persist the justified high-water with commits. After an unproved restart H is merely observed wall advance and can undercount; max preserves a surviving local bound but cannot repair undercount, server-clock steps/drift or stale/error-bound ambiguity. Raw Date.now is never compared directly with server lease UTC: a constant ±24h client skew cancels in W−W0; a later jump is handled below. Do not call Thi + H a certified real-time bound after restart. Independent authenticated proof of expiry still refuses20. **No CLOCK PASS until W5 states sufficient bounds**; retaining 24h/64 does not supply them or reopen the settled bounded-rule exposure below.
**Last valid / first invalid:** an allowance that has not been invalidated, with H ≤ 86,400,000 and U + N ≤ 64, permits a batch of N new operation slots only if every underlying lease endpoint also permits it. Exactly the final millisecond and slot 64 are valid; the following millisecond or slot 65 refuses state 20. Both ends of the issued sequence/time interval remain inclusive. A batch crossing any end refuses in full, consumes no sequence/budget, keeps entered values and says no Saved.
**What consumes the budget:** newly durably committed athlete operations, including explicit correction/replacement operations, consume slots. Receipt/pull processing, exact resend, rendering and token refresh do not. A multi-op session consumes its full operation count atomically; a declared durable draft follows its own mechanism and is not silently converted into an operation. A commit abort consumes nothing. Never reserve a smaller slot count by splitting an atomic action.
**Renewal:** reconcile acknowledged and pending history, device sequence/head, standing and valid issued capabilities; then activate a fresh checkpoint in one transaction. Preserve every original outbox envelope and previous valid lease proof needed for delivery. Do not renumber/re-sign pending operations or turn a WAITING parent relationship into a transport-sequence gap. After detected restore/conflict, reconcile or re-enrol under the published recovery contract before resetting counters.
**Why these numbers:** 24 observed hours permits an ordinary day between connections; 64 slots permits a modest Today workload without using private usage statistics. Neither is measured optimal. A smaller allowance reconnects more often; a larger allowance permits more unreconciled work. Sol retained both numbers; 64 is only a client-policy cap, not a server/global-loss bound. Cowork verifies v1.1 against the four recorded corrections before marking the refinement DONE. Do not silently relabel this operating allowance as a stronger security or loss bound.
