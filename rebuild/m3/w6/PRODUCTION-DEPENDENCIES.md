## T1/R1 interface requirements; no guessed time constants or wire

T1 must pin where the server sample occurs relative to I/O/signing and how a qualified same-execution client duration bounds response age. Specify milliseconds/UTC conversion, inclusive endpoints, error/rate/step limits and unavailable-profile refusal. The current30,000ms observed challenge timeout proves neither real RTT nor UTC accuracy. Synchronous frame encryption authenticates a later sample; it does not make encryption/IDB completion instantaneous.
