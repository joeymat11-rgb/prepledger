Two sessions on one day stay distinct; no date-key overwrite, repeated-exercise workaround or arrival-order interpretation of concurrency.
Keep prescribed loads/targets/effort and actual performance in separate fields. `exercise.wSets` is a prescribed vector, never an alias for performed weights. Do not add a vector to a legacy entry and assume the old writer consumed it: the executable collision witness disproves that. This view is rebuildable read state. Reopening, syncing, correcting or replaying facts MUST NOT call `completeSession` again to mint receipts, mutate the current plan or apply an adjustment. Any engine transition/proposal is evaluated once against its declared basis and committed only through the existing authorized operation/consent/plan-transaction path. An acknowledged workout fact is not consent to a plan change. Derived caches may be discarded/rebuilt; immutable originals and agreed plan effects may not.
