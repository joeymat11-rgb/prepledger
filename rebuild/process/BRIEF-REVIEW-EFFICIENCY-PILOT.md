## 3. Review packet and measurements
One packet per exact candidate: item/owner; base and candidate commit; accepted brief and interface versions; allowed/protected paths; relevant callers; user-visible claim; declared risk category; required gate/environment matrix; guarded evidence locations; unresolved obligations; NEXT.
Give the shadow reviewer sufficient source access but withhold old verdicts, corrected diffs, answer keys and builder advocacy. Historical cases familiar to either reviewer are calibration only, not blind holdouts.
Each role records actual exit status and full required gate inventory. A missing adapter/test, skipped required job, truncated verdict, wrong checkout or stale receipt cannot become PASS.
Keep the full current conformance/oracle/selftest/strict, effective mutation/restoration, Windows/Linux, source-derived expectation, private-custody, real-backend and physical/soak gates wherever required. No test-only simulation substitutes for the named environment.
Record within the existing checkpoint: comparison candidate/model/effort/prompt; included usage or attributable cost source; preparation/review/integration/rework time; consequential misses; false positives/rework; harmful suggestions; owner actions; execution/acceptance state.
Separate input, cache and output categories when available. Shared account percentages and list-price estimates are not invoices or task-attributable spend. Do not double-count reasoning output or repeated cumulative usage events.
Use paired per-change comparison including retries, context preparation and rework; record the temporary shadow-trial overhead separately from projected steady operation. Unknown costs remain UNKNOWN.
The existing 20% attributable-efficiency target is a pilot hypothesis. No extra consequential misses, weakened checks or added owner relay burden is acceptable. A missed lost-fact/unconsented-action/untrue-output/hidden-commit/privacy/auth issue, false PASS, unauthorized mutation or invented expectation stops expansion.
Tests establish behavior, not whether an unratified product rule is correct. Retain owner rule authority and existing dispute/milestone review. Agreement between models is not ground truth or statistical independence.
